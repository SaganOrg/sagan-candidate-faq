Candidate FAQ

Answers to the questions candidates ask us most often about applications, uploads, and AI interviews.

Please read first

Sagan receives a large number of applications. We cannot answer individual status questions or troubleshoot applications by email. If we want to move forward with your application, we will contact you.

Questions

What is the status of my application?

We do not provide individual status updates, timelines, or interview feedback. If your application moves forward, Sagan will contact you by email. There is nothing you need to do while you wait.

Did you receive my application, video, or interview?

If the page showed “Your application is with Sagan” or another completion message, we have it and no further action is needed. We do not send individual confirmation emails.

If you stopped part-way, your answers are saved. Use the link in our most recent email to continue where you left off.

Can I retake my AI interview?

Yes. Open the same interview link again. You can take the interview up to four times for each application. After your third attempt, the page tells you that you can re-do it one more time.

What happens after I use all four attempts?

After four attempts, the interview closes for that application and cannot be submitted again. You can still continue the rest of your application, such as your video.

If you believe this is an error or need special assistance, email recruitment@getsagan.com. If you had technical difficulties during your attempts, mention this in your email. These cases are reviewed individually.

My AI interview had a technical problem. What should I do?

Hundreds of candidates complete Sagan's AI interview each day without technical problems. If yours is not working, open the link in Chrome or Safari, not inside the Gmail, LinkedIn, or another app, where the microphone is often blocked. Allow microphone access, and find a quiet place with a stable internet connection. Try another browser or device before starting your next attempt.

If the interview did not receive any audio from your microphone, the page tells you so, and no attempt is used. If you have used all four attempts, see What happens after I use all four attempts?

My video or file will not upload. Can I email it?

No. Try again from the link in our most recent email, in Chrome or Safari, or on another device. If the video is stored in Google Photos, Google Drive, or another cloud app, save it to your phone first and choose it again, or record a new one.

Do not email attachments, Google Drive links, Dropbox links, or WeTransfer links to this inbox. They will not be added to your application.

Can I get more time or have an expired link reopened?

Your progress is saved, so you can come back and finish your application while it is open. Use the link in our most recent email.

If a page says “This link is no longer active”, the invitation it came from has expired or been replaced. If you received a newer email from Sagan, use the link in that one. Otherwise, there is nothing further you need to do. If a page says “This application is closed”, that role is no longer taking applications.

A sign-in link for your Sagan profile works for one hour, and only once. If it has expired, ask for a new one on the sign-in page. This inbox cannot grant extensions or reopen expired links.

Can I send a new résumé or change my information by email?

No. Sign in to your Sagan profile at my.saganrecruitment.com with your email address. There you can update your résumé, intro video, email address, WhatsApp number, and other details. This inbox cannot update your résumé, email address, phone number, or application details.

How do I apply for another Sagan role?

Apply through the application page for that role. You are considered for every role you meet the requirements for, so there is no need to apply for the same role twice. We do not accept résumés or job applications by email.

Can I email Sagan for more help?

This candidate inbox is automated, and replies are not monitored. Sending another email will not change your application or move it forward.
