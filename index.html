Candidate FAQ

Answers to the questions candidates ask us most often about applications, uploads, and AI interviews.

Please read first

Sagan receives a large number of applications. We cannot answer individual status questions or troubleshoot applications by email. If we want to move forward with your application, we will contact you.

Questions

What is the status of my application?

We do not provide individual status updates, timelines, or interview feedback. If your application moves forward, Sagan will contact you. There is nothing you need to do while you wait.

Did you receive my application, video, or interview?

If the application or interview page showed a completion or success message, no further action is needed. We do not send individual confirmation emails.

Can I retake my AI interview?

Use the same interview link. You can complete up to three attempts.

What happens after I use all three attempts?

After the third attempt, the interview cannot be opened again. Attempts are not restored for browser, device, connection, camera, or microphone problems. This inbox cannot reset or reopen attempts. If Sagan needs anything else, we will contact you.

My AI interview had a technical problem. What should I do?

Hundreds of candidates complete Sagan's AI interview each day without technical problems. If yours is not working, first check your browser, device, internet connection, and camera and microphone permissions. Use another browser or device before starting your next attempt. Attempts used during a local technical problem cannot be restored.

My video or file will not upload. Can I email it?

Try the original upload link in a different browser or on another device. Do not email attachments, Google Drive links, Dropbox links, or WeTransfer links to this inbox. They will not be added to your application.

Can I get more time or have an expired link reopened?

Complete each step while its link is active. This inbox cannot grant extensions or reopen expired links.

Can I send a new résumé or change my information by email?

No. Use the application page or candidate profile if it allows changes. This inbox cannot update your résumé, email address, phone number, or application details.

How do I apply for another Sagan role?

Apply through the application page for that role. We do not accept résumés or job applications by email.

Can I email Sagan for more help?

This candidate inbox is automated, and replies are not monitored. Sending another email will not change your application or move it forward.
