SAGAN CANDIDATE FAQ
Last updated 20 August 2026


PLEASE READ FIRST

Sagan receives a large number of applications. We cannot answer individual
status questions or troubleshoot applications by email. If we want to move
forward with your application, we will contact you.


WHAT IS THE STATUS OF MY APPLICATION?

We do not provide individual status updates, timelines, or interview
feedback. If your application moves forward, Sagan will contact you. There
is nothing you need to do while you wait.


DID YOU RECEIVE MY APPLICATION, VIDEO, OR INTERVIEW?

If the application or interview page showed a completion or success message,
no further action is needed. We do not send individual confirmation emails.


CAN I RETAKE MY AI INTERVIEW?

Use the same interview link. You can complete up to three attempts.


WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I USE ALL THREE ATTEMPTS?

After the third attempt, the interview cannot be opened again. Attempts are
not restored for browser, device, connection, camera, or microphone
problems. This inbox cannot reset or reopen attempts. If Sagan needs
anything else, we will contact you.


MY AI INTERVIEW HAD A TECHNICAL PROBLEM. WHAT SHOULD I DO?

Hundreds of candidates complete Sagan's AI interview each day without
technical problems. If yours is not working, first check your browser,
device, internet connection, and camera and microphone permissions. Use
another browser or device before starting your next attempt. Attempts used
during a local technical problem cannot be restored.


MY VIDEO OR FILE WILL NOT UPLOAD. CAN I EMAIL IT?

Try the original upload link in a different browser or on another device. Do
not email attachments, Google Drive links, Dropbox links, or WeTransfer
links to this inbox. They will not be added to your application.


CAN I GET MORE TIME OR HAVE AN EXPIRED LINK REOPENED?

Complete each step while its link is active. This inbox cannot grant
extensions or reopen expired links.


CAN I SEND A NEW RESUME OR CHANGE MY INFORMATION BY EMAIL?

No. Use the application page or candidate profile if it allows changes. This
inbox cannot update your resume, email address, phone number, or application
details.


HOW DO I APPLY FOR ANOTHER SAGAN ROLE?

Apply through the application page for that role. We do not accept resumes
or job applications by email.


CAN I EMAIL SAGAN FOR MORE HELP?

This candidate inbox is automated, and replies are not monitored. Sending
another email will not change your application or move it forward.
